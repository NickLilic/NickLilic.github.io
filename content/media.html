Media

Alongside his clinical work, Nick teaches and writes on ENT, head and neck surgery for GPs and medical students. Below are recent podcast episodes covering the problems he is asked about most — neck lumps and the blocked nose — recorded for a primary care audience, but useful listening for anyone wanting to understand these conditions better.

Neck lumps

The Specialist GP · Episode 22 · 31 July 2026 · with Dr Nick Lilic and Dr John Chaplin

Following a 62 year old kaumātua's journey from his first presentation in primary care through to specialist referral. The episode covers the risk factors and changing epidemiology of head and neck cancer, recognising red flags, appropriate investigation and referral, the value of a neck lump clinic, and the role primary care plays in improving outcomes for Māori and Pacific peoples.

Listen on The Specialist GP

Blocked and congested noses

Goodfellow Unit Podcast · 9 August 2023 · 22 min · with Dr Nick Lilic

An overview of the common clinical syndromes that cause nasal congestion — rhinitis, sinusitis and structural problems inside the nose — how they are assessed, what conservative management looks like, and when surgery is worth considering.
